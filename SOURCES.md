## Research rules

1. Pin the product version and capture the retrieval date.
2. Describe observable outcomes in original neutral wording.
3. Prefer black-box fixtures over implementation inference.
4. Record uncertainty and differences.
5. Do not paste upstream source, documentation prose or assets into this repository.
6. Do not use proprietary or leaked material.
7. Update `project-baseline.json` and conformance fixtures together when rebasing.
