# Community code of conduct

We are committed to a professional, inclusive and technically rigorous community.

Participants must:

- treat people with respect and critique ideas or evidence rather than individuals;
- make room for different backgrounds, communication styles and experience levels;
- disclose conflicts of interest and relevant implementation provenance;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and sexualized conduct;
- protect user data, credentials and vulnerability information;
- represent compatibility, benchmarks and security evidence honestly.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content, restrict participation or ban participants when necessary
to protect the project and community. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, applied
consistently and provide an appeal route that does not require contact with the original decision-maker.
